# Survey paper modules

Use the shared backbone and insert these modules after Key Insight.

## Report order

1. Basic Information and Coverage
2. Research Problem
3. Key Insight
4. Taxonomy (`data-section="taxonomy"`)
5. Open Problems and Trends (`data-section="open-problems"`)
6. Critical Analysis
7. Summary and Evaluation

## Taxonomy

- State corpus size, time span, inclusion/exclusion criteria, and search/screening method when available.
- Explain why the classification axes are meaningful rather than merely convenient.
- Define each category, representative works, strengths, weaknesses, and best-fit regime.
- Use a comparison table when the same dimensions repeat across categories.
- Identify overlaps and works that do not fit cleanly; these test the taxonomy.

For each major direction, cover the mechanism family, progression of ideas, current bottleneck, and evidence used by the survey to support its synthesis. Do not turn the section into a list of paper titles.

## Open Problems and Trends

- Separate trends observed in the reviewed corpus from author predictions.
- Name unresolved technical or evaluation bottlenecks.
- Explain which proposed direction is most promising and what evidence would validate it.
- Check whether the survey's cutoff date or venue/language selection biases the apparent trend.

Critical analysis must examine coverage, taxonomy stability, missing directions, and author perspective. Inspect any official survey artifact or quantitative-synthesis code read-only when it materially supports the taxonomy; do not pretend a single implementation validates the survey's full coverage.
